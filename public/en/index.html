AeroEscuela: flight training simulator.

Bring real training to your school on an ordinary computer: six-degrees-of-freedom physics validated against the manufacturer's manual, a virtual instructor who guides every lesson, risk-free emergencies, GPS navigation with RNAV and ILS approaches, and real Venezuelan airports with their terrain and satellite imagery.
