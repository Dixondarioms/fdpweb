Adminia. Your store, in bolívars and dollars.

Sales, inventory, invoicing and tax ledgers for hardware stores, auto parts shops, corner stores and retailers in Venezuela. It installs on a computer in your store and keeps working even when the internet goes down.

Your whole store, in one system.

Every sale at the register updates inventory, goes into the sales ledger and shows up in the reports, with no manual re-entry.

1

Point of sale

Sell with a barcode scanner, by unit, meter, kilo or package. Split payments in cash (Bs and USD), mobile payment, bank transfer, card terminal, Zelle and Binance, with change in Bs or USD.

2

Prices in dollars and bolívars

Official BCV exchange rate fetched automatically, or entered by hand when offline. Price lists (retail, wholesale, contractor) and bulk adjustments by supplier, brand or category.

3

Pre-printed invoice books

Invoice and control numbers in order with no gaps, VAT by rate and IGTF tax, and credit and debit notes with their own books.

4

Cash register

Open with a float in Bs and USD, record withdrawals and expenses, and close with a count by payment method: expected, counted and the difference.

5

Inventory

Stock levels and a ledger of every movement, adjustments with a reason, alerts for products below minimum, and physical counts with a scanner or a phone.

6

Purchasing and suppliers

Goods receiving, purchase orders suggested from minimum stock levels, and accounts payable with due dates.

7

Customer credit

Credit limit and terms per customer, sales on credit, payments in any currency and accounts receivable.

8

Tax ledgers and withholdings

Sales and purchase ledgers with Excel export, and VAT withholdings with their receipt and the TXT file for SENIAT, Venezuela's tax authority.

9

Reports, users and backups

Sales, profit and best sellers; roles so cashiers don't see costs; and an automatic daily backup, also copied off the computer.
