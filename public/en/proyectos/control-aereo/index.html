Air control and interception. Train your controllers.

Simulator for training controllers, with radar tracks, flight identification and voice communications powered by local artificial intelligence. The instructor prepares each exercise and evaluates performance.

Intensive practice, without tying up the real system.

Repeatable scenarios so every controller can practice until mastering the procedure.

1

Radar tracks

Radar display with simulated tracks whose headings, altitudes and speeds change in real time.

2

Flight identification

Aircraft identification and tracking practice in scenarios with varied traffic.

3

Voice communications

Students talk to simulated pilots; speech recognition and replies are processed on the computer itself.

4

Interception

Guidance exercises toward a target, with headings and times calculated by the simulator.

5

Instructor station

Creates the scenarios, controls the traffic and follows what each student does.

6

Evaluation

Complete exercise log to review every decision with the student.
