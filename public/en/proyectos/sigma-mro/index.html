SIGMA MRO. Your fleet, up to date and under control.

Aviation maintenance management for maintenance organizations, flight schools and operators of any manufacturer and model of airplane or helicopter. Runs on your own equipment, 100% cloud-free.

All your maintenance, in a single system.

Every module shares the same information, so what happens in the hangar is instantly reflected in inventory, purchasing and due dates.

1

Fleet and components

Aircraft, engines and components with their limits by hours, cycles or calendar, and alerts before every due date.

2

Maintenance program

Scheduled inspections and recurring tasks calculated automatically for each aircraft.

3

Work orders

Tasks with technician sign-off, inspection and return to service, with the complete history of every job.

4

Hangar board

At a glance: which aircraft are in service, in maintenance or waiting for parts.

5

Inventory and purchasing

Stock, serialized parts, requests and purchase orders connected to the hangar work.

6

AD/SB directives

Control of airworthiness directives and service bulletins, with compliance per aircraft.

7

Documents and personnel

Manuals, certificates and staff licenses in one place, with their expiration dates.

8

Engine trends

Flight log and engine parameter charts, with automatic monitoring that warns you if something goes out of range.

AI

Local AI assistant

Ask in plain language, also by voice, and get answers based on your manuals and records without sending data to the internet.
