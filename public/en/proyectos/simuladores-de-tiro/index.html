Anti-aircraft shooting. From detection to impact.

Full sequence

Preparation, target identification, lock-on and firing, in the same order as the real procedure.

2

Targets in flight

Airplanes and helicopters with varied routes and maneuvers: passes over the position, low-level flights and point defense.

3

Weather and time of day

Day, night, rain or storm exercises, to train in difficult conditions.

4

Realistic terrain

Savannas, hills and mountains with relief and vegetation, where the target appears and hides just like on real terrain.

5

Instructor console

Exercise catalog, a profile for each operator and real-time tracking of the launcher, the telescope and the aircraft.

6

Automatic scoring

A score at the end and a step-by-step timeline to correct mistakes.
