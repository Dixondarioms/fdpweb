Ritmo Diario

A support app to organize the daily wellbeing of people with frequent pain, fatigue or other chronic symptoms that need constant tracking.

  • Daily log of symptoms, pain, sleep and mood
  • Treatments, hydration, gentle exercises and reminders
  • Easy mode and larger text for your most tiring days

An emotional wellbeing app that helps you keep no contact, strengthen your healing process and build self-care habits day by day.

  • Tools to keep no contact, and a strength button

  • Guided breathing, meditation and SOS tools
