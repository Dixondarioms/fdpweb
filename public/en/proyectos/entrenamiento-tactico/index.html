FDP Simuladores · Tactical training

Tactical and strategic training. Decide like a General Staff.

Turn-based operations simulator for the classroom: students form one side's General Staff, plan and conduct the campaign on real maps against another group or an automated opponent, and receive an evaluation at the end.

Request a demo What's included
Mode
Turn-based, in the classroom
Sides
Blue and Red
Opponent
Another group or automated
Connection
Local network, no internet

The classroom becomes a command post.

Each student plays a role, every order has consequences and the instructor controls the pace of the exercise.

1

General Staff roles

Personnel, intelligence, operations, air defense, logistics and communications: each student issues only the orders of their own branch.

2

Instructor and observers

The instructor sets the problem, fixes the schedule, resolves the turns and evaluates; observers see everything without intervening.

3

Fog of war

Each side sees only what its own assets detect, and the server rejects any action outside its role.

4

Real and fictional maps

Theaters of operations on real cartography with satellite imagery, or fictional maps for academic exercises.

5

Tactical graphics

Phase lines, axes of advance, assembly areas and checkpoints drawn on the map and shared with the side.

6

Opponent and evaluation

An automated opponent when there is no second group, and a final report to review every decision.
